# boiade (Livornese) research log

Date: 2026-11-25. Sources prefix `src-li-`.

## Read (data only)
- Catarsi, Dizionario Livornese (livornese.altervista.org): home, Fonti, trascrizione pages and letters A-Z via ascii=65..90 (curl; WebFetch for first pages). Main lexical and orthographic source.
- Treccani, Dialetti toscani (Enciclopedia dell'Italiano): pisano-livornese phonetics, dé as flag feature.
- it.wikipedia Dialetti_toscani (first 100,000 chars): gorgia, ir, impersonal si, Tuscan syntax.
- Livorno Magazine: Vernacolando, Modi di dire e proverbi (about 150 sayings, many crude).
- Daniela Lenzi vernacolario; Pescheria Arbulla vocabolario; it.wikipedia Il Vernacoliere.

## Decisions
- Orthography: Catarsi's (dé, c'ho, accented infinitives, un). Elided intervocalic c shown with apostrophe but only on frequent words.
- Localization: city of Livorno only; Piombino forms (nenne) excluded; Pisan/rural excluded.
- Excluded on purpose: Catarsi's words for stingy based on religion/town, slurs (stoppone, paccherino, manfruito, dòddo, marrocchino), "ebreo/lucchese/rabbino".
- Proverbs from Livorno Magazine: kept only non-stereotyping ones; meanings are our reading, marked unverified (no glosses on the page). Skipped those about Pisans, bike theft, disability and the unglossable.
- Only blasphemy entry: Maremma cane (minced form documented by Catarsi). No unminced form generated.
- Wiktionary/Crusca not usable, so little verb paradigm data; table in profile built from attested forms and itwiki.

## Alias check
"deh" is not appropriate: Catarsi says deh is an old exhortation unrelated to Livornese dé; Livorno Magazine says the same. Remove "deh" and "boia deh" from the catalog (they are also generic Tuscan/literary and would misdirect). "boia de" / "boiade'" are acceptable accent-less spellings. Catalog meaning ok except "dé particle" (it is an interjection).

## Doubts
- Meanings of Livorno Magazine proverbs; Garbulla-only items (arrivà dopo la porvere, bimbo da puppa).
- Verb forms for noi/voi/loro not directly attested beyond impersonal si.
