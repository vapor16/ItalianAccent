Decisions
- Orthography: Academy 'grafia ofiçiâ'. Letter values taken from English Wikipedia; Academy subpages unreadable. Source spellings vary (cû/cù, belìn/belin/bellin); Wikiquote and popular sites are not in Academy spelling and are kept verbatim in proverbs.json.
- Localization: Genoa only. belin also occurs in Oltregiogo, lower Lunigiana, Tabarchino (Carloforte, Calasetta), Menton-Nice; belina (f.) is Spezzino; abbellinou Savonese.
- Catalog: alias 'belinn-a' is not attested as a Genoese form of belin (suggest removing). Word and area association correct.
- No blasphemy documented; none invented.
- Examples are AI-composed; a few forms there (zà, scimisce, desmentegâ, ùrtima, uzâ, mesciâla, parolasce, fonçiona, aplicaçion) are NOT from sources and need native review.

Doubts: verb table garbled in itwiki; imperative/future/progressive undocumented; popular-site items unverified; 'figgeu' as an exclamation low reliability.
