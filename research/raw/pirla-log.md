## Decisions
- Orthography: classical Milanese (oeu, u, doubled long vowels), not modern ö/ü; documented alternatives in profile section 2. Deviates from the lead's ö/ü hint because sources and Wikipedia use the classical standard.
- Interrogative inversion: documented only as archaic (Voeurela?); profile uses clitic-before-verb questions.
- Proverbs keep the spelling of their (low-reliability) source page; a variant of 'Offellee fa el tò mestee' seen only in a search summary was left out.
- lògia (misogynistic) and terun (regional slur) kept with generation_allowed false.
- No blasphemy documented; none generated.
