## Decisions
- Orthography: RID/Sanga as in the thesis (ö ü è é ò ó ṡ s-c). Aspirated h written only as marked option.
- Localization: urban/lowland Bergamo, no valley forms.
- Accent: lemma pòta; potà is a positional allomorph (stress shift next to stressed final syllable; thesis citing Sanga 1987:78) and a rare spelling (Wikizionario). Display Potà is attested but secondary.
- Lead's "h for Latin f/s": only s>h documented; f stays f.
- Catalog meaning "originally vulgar female genitals" confirmed (Tiraboschi, Wikizionario, thesis). Area Lombardia/Bergamo confirmed. Pòta also reported for Brescian (unverified).

## Doubts
- Everyday vocabulary beyond the corpus is thin; examples lean on Italianized technical words and pattern-based imperatives (fa, ciapa, parla, cüntèm, lasèm, pensóm). 2sg/2pl of ndà undocumented.
- Profanity is mostly attested exclamations; no documented insults. Pötarga unverified.
- Tiraboschi proverbs kept in his 1875 spelling; modernisation not attempted.
