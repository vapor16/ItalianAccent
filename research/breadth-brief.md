# Breadth-first brief: minimal functional profile (v0.1.0)

Goal: make ONE mode genuinely usable, authentically and with sources, while spending as little budget as possible. Enrichment comes later.

Read: `docs/adding-a-dialect.md` (the contract) and `research/agent-brief.md` (tools, write scope, quality rules — its "Targets" section is replaced by the targets below), plus `schemas/*.schema.json` and your entry in `skills/italianaccent/references/catalog.json`. As a calibrated model of the expected format/quality, skim ONE pilot profile: `skills/italianaccent/references/dialects/daje/profile.md` (first 120 lines) and a few entries of its lexicon/proverbs/profanity JSON.

Research efficiently: 6–12 good sources (Wikipedia it/regional editions for grammar, Wiktionary/Wikizionario, public-domain dictionaries on archive.org/Wikisource/Google Books, academic pages, Wikiquote proverbs, reputable local sites for slang/profanity with low reliability). Prefer pages that give grammar tables and word lists. Never invent forms, rules, proverbs or sources; skip what you cannot document.

Write ONLY: `skills/italianaccent/references/dialects/<id>/{metadata.json,profile.md,lexicon.json,proverbs.json,profanity.json,examples.md}`, `research/raw/<id>-sources.json`, `research/raw/<id>-log.md` (short). Do NOT write sources.md (generated by script). No git. No shared files.

Minimal functional targets (enrichment goals beyond this are welcome only if cheap):
- profile.md: all 15 contract sections, 120–250 lines, real documented grammar (articles, pronouns, key verb paradigms, negation, characteristic syntax, orthography), core lexicon table (~50 items), profanity palette by level, 8–12 quick proverbs/idioms, intensity guide, transformation guide (AI-illustrative), contamination guard vs neighbouring varieties.
- lexicon.json ≥ 80 entries (incl. ≥10 interjections, ≥10 colloquial expressions).
- proverbs.json ≥ 20 (proverbs + idioms).
- profanity.json ≥ 15 (uncensored, intensity 1–5, friendly/hostile, blasphemies flagged with "blasphemy": true and intensity ≥4).
- examples.md: required header; reference prompts 1, 2, 7, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18 (see tests/behavioral/reference-prompts.md), answers 4–8 lines, fully in dialect at intensity 5 / vulgarity 5, correct untouched code; then a 2-item "Technical examples" section.
- metadata.json: schema-valid, "version":"0.1.0", "maturity":"experimental" and "available":true only if the core is authentic and sourced; otherwise "planned"/false with reasons in known_limitations. "reviews":[]. resources include sources.md. region exactly as in catalog.json. identifier_word documented with attestation; if the catalog's mode word or area association is wrong/unattested, say so in known_limitations and in your final report.

Finish: `python3 scripts/validate.py --profile <id>` — the only acceptable remaining error is "missing sources.md". Final reply ≤ 12 lines: counts, orthography/localization decisions, catalog corrections needed, doubts.
