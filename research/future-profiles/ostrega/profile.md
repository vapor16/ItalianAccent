# Ostrega — Venetian of Venice (Veneziano)

> Experimental v0.1.0, breadth-first profile. No native-speaker review. Grammar rests on Wikipedia (it/en/vec) and Wiktionary Venetan; the vulgar lexicon is thin and partly low-reliability (see section 9).

## 1. Identity & scope

- Variety: **Veneziano**, the Venetian of the city of Venice (centro storico, lagoon). It is the prestige/koine base of Venetian (Italo-Romance, Gallo-Italic-adjacent, "Veneto" group) and the basis of Trevisan; Padua, Rovigo and Vicenza urban speech is related to it ([src-vec-itwiki-dialetto-veneziano]).
- Region: Veneto. The profile does **not** cover Veronese, Bellunese, Trevisan countryside, Friulian-influenced varieties, or the Venetian of Trieste/Istria/Brazil.
- Identifier word: **òstrega!** = "gosh!, wow!"; literally *ostrica* (oyster, [src-vec-wiktionary-venetan], Boerio gives *ostrega* as the animal). Italian dictionaries and the it.wikipedia article on *bestemmia* treat *ostrega/ostregheta* as softened exclamations; the word is **pan-Venetian, not Venice-only**. It is kept for Venice because the catalog localizes the mode there and because the Venice dictionary tradition (Boerio) records the noun.
- Local norm followed: the Venetian of Venice as described by it.wikipedia "Dialetto veneziano": **ti** as 2sg pronoun and clitic (other Veneto dialects: *te*), **nialtri / vialtri** for we/you-pl, imperfect of *esser* with **g-** (*gero, geri, gera*), *xe* for "is".

## 2. Orthography

- **Adopted: traditional Venetian spelling consistent with the Grafia Veneta Unitaria (GVU, commission directed by M. Cortelazzo, 1995; later GVIM-DECA/GVIM 2017) — but with plain `l`, not `ł`.** Reason: GVU `ł` (evanescent l) hurts readability in chat output. `la`, `le`, `lu`, `el` replace `ła`, `łe`, `łu`, `eło`. Writers may add `ł` at the user's request (`ła xe`).
- `x` = voiced /z/ (xe, cusina, xente); `s` = voiceless; no `ss`. `z` is kept only where GVU/tradition has it for /ts/-/dz/ words (zorno, zà); `ç` is not used.
- `ch`, `gh` always hard (ghe, che, ciò is `ci-`: *cia, cio, ciu* = /tʃ/). `sc` is written as in Italian only for /ʃ/-less clusters; /s-tʃ/ is `s-c` (s-ciopo) — rare, avoid.
- **No double consonants**: *pala*, *casa*, *bela*, *tuto*, *caro*, not Italian *palla/tutto*. Apparent geminates in proverbs below are inherited from the source spelling only if the source has them.
- Accents mark stress on oxytones and past participles: *magnà, catà, visto* (no accent), *xé* is optional (`xe` preferred), 2pl present *magné, vardé, gavé*, infinitives lose final -e (*magnar, saver, dir, far*).
- Apostrophe: `no'l` (= *no el*), `l'è` not used for Venice (`el xe`), `'ndove`/`ndove` both attested ([src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]), `go'l`. Articles may elide: `l'amigo`, `l'acqua`.

## 3. Phonology visible in writing

- Loss of geminates (above) and **apocope** of final -e/-o after n, r, l (can, pan, vin, mar, bon, magnar, mal, ben) ([src-vec-enwiki-venetian]); plurals are mostly unmarked on nouns/adjectives, number is on article and clitic (*el can / i can*).
- **Evanescent l**: not rendered in the plain-l spelling, but it explains vocalic readings (*el, la, le, lu*).
- Palatal/velar softening and Venetian vowels: `z` voiced: *zorno* "giorno", *zà* "già"; `x` is fricative /z/: *xe, xente* (tradition also writes *ze, zente*).
- Italian *gl*/*gn*: *fiol* (figlio), *fioi* (kids), *gnente*, *gnanca*, *mejo/megio* "meglio", *mègio* in it.wikipedia; final -n velar: *pirón* (forchetta), *man*, *pan*.
- Italian *-zione* → *-sion* (*aplicasion*, *configurasion*); *c+e,i* → /s/ vs /ts/: avoid unattested spellings.

## 4. Morphology

- **Articles**: m.sg *el* (before vowel *l'*), f.sg *la*, m.pl *i*, f.pl *le*; indefinite *un/on*, *na* (f.) ([src-vec-enwiki-venetian], [src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]). Prepositions combine: *del, al, dal, sul, col* (m.); feminine forms stay analytic in writing: *de la, a la, da la, su la, co la*.
- **Subject pronouns (tonic)**: *mi, ti, lu, ela, nialtri* (also *nualtri*), *vialtri*, *lori/lore* ([src-vec-itwiki-dialetto-veneziano]).
- **Subject clitics (obligatory 2sg, 3sg, 3pl; 1sg/1pl/2pl none)**: 2sg *ti/te*, 3sg m *el*, 3sg f *la*, 3pl *i* (m) / *le* (f) ([src-vec-itwiki-lingua-veneta], [src-vec-enwiki-venetian]). Pattern: **mi go, ti ti ga, lu el ga, ela la ga, nialtri gavemo, vialtri gavé, lori i ga**. 3sg and 3pl verbs are identical: *el va / i va* ([src-vec-itwiki-lingua-veneta]).
- **Emphatic particle `a`** before a clitic: *A te dormi mai!* "ma non dormi mai!" ([src-vec-itwiki-lingua-veneta]).
- Datives/locatives: *ghe* = "to him/her/them" and "there": *ghe xe* "c'è/ci sono", *ghe vol* "ci vuole" ([src-vec-wiktionary-venetan], [src-vec-wikiquote-proverbi]).
- Possessives: *me, to, so* (invariable-ish: *me mare, to pare*); not verified in the read sources — keep minimal.
- Demonstratives: *sto/sta/sti/ste* (*sto codice*), *quel/quela*; keep to these.

## 5. Verbs

Present (Venice, plain l; forms cross-checked with it.wikipedia and Wiktionary Venetan, some paradigm cells unverified):

| | esser | aver | far | ndar | dir |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mi | son / so | go | fazo | vago | digo |
| ti ti | xe | ga | fa | va | dixi |
| lu el / ela la | xe | ga | fa | va | dixe |
| nialtri | semo | gavemo | femo | ndemo | disemo |
| vialtri | sì | gavé | fe | ndé | dì |
| lori i | xe | ga | fa | va | dixe |

- `esser`: *mi son/so, ti ti xe/ti sì, el xe, nialtri semo, vialtri sì, i xe*; imperfect *gero, geri, gera, gerimo* (Venice) ([src-vec-itwiki-dialetto-veneziano], [src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]).
- `aver`: *mi go, ti ti ga, el ga, nialtri gavemo, vialtri gavé, i ga* ([src-vec-enwiki-venetian], [src-vec-itwiki-lingua-veneta]).
- `far, dir, ndar, voler, poder, saver` (cells marked unverified in the lexicon): *mi voio, ti ti vol, el vol; el pol (Q: "tuto la puol"); mi so, ti ti sa*.
- **Past**: only the **compound past** (*ga* + participle): *mi go magnà, ti ga visto, el ga fato*. No passato remoto. -ar participle in **-à** (*magnà, catà, parlà*), -ir in **-ìo/-ì**, irregular *visto, dito, fato*. Reflexives take *aver* ([src-vec-enwiki-venetian]).
- Imperfect -ar: *magnava*; 1pl *magnàvimo*; futures in -arà (*el farà, el magnarà*); conditional *saria, magnaria* ([src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]).
- **Progressive**: *esser drio + infinitive*: *el xe drio manjar / drio magnar* "sta mangiando" ([src-vec-enwiki-venetian]).
- Obligation: *ghe vol* "ci vuole", *bexogna / bisogna*, *cogner*; "have to" *go da + inf* (unverified).
- Imperative: *vien, varda, speta, ciapa, scolta* (2sg = bare stem; sources give *varda*, *speta*, *ciapa*, *scolta* as verbs).

## 6. Syntax

- Clitic doubling is the rule: *Giorgio el vien* ([src-vec-itwiki-lingua-veneta]); tonic pronoun optional: *(mi) go fame*.
- **Negation**: preverbal `no` placed before the clitic+verb: *mi no go, ti no ti ga, el no 'l xe → no'l xe*, reinforced by *miga* ("mica"): *No'l sarìa miga sta drio parlarte* ([src-vec-enwiki-venetian]); *A no te dormi mai* ([src-vec-itwiki-lingua-veneta]). Double negation: *no ... gnente / gnanca / mai*.
- **Interrogatives**: enclitic inversion for 2sg in traditional Venetian — *Dìtu, Sìtu, Atu visto?, Vatu 'ndove?* — but Venice and city dialects prefer **dissociation**: *Ti ga visto? Te dizi par davero?* ([src-vec-itwiki-lingua-veneta]). 3sg: *Pàrlelo?* (traditional). Use dissociated forms by default.
- SVO; VSO in questions. Subjunctive is frequent; subordinators are doubled (*chi che, che*) ([src-vec-enwiki-venetian]).
- Quantifiers: *asè* "molto", *masa* "troppo", *pena* "appena" ([src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]).
- Tags and fillers: *ciò* (call/address), *ara* (look!), *no?* — unverified in the read sources.

## 8. Discourse markers & interjections

- **òstrega!** surprise, admiration, annoyance, disbelief (tone decides); **ostregheta!** diminutive, softer ([src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]).
- **ocio!** "attento!"; **mósca!** "zitto!"; **'sa?** "che? perché?"; **ceste!** "che vuoi farci" (c'est la vie); **dacordo!** OK; **grassie** thanks; **benvegnuo!** welcome; **àmen** ([src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]).
- **ciò!** address/attention; **ara!** look!; **ma va'!** disbelief (unverified).
- Starter: *Òstrega, ...* ; end tag: *no?*

## 9. Profanity palette by level (0–5)

Documentation of Venetian vulgarity is the weakest part of this profile: only the items below are sourced; blasphemies are **not documented** in the read sources and are therefore **not included** (do not invent *bestemmie*).

- **0–1**: *òstrega, ostregheta, ocio, mósca* (euphemistic; it.wikipedia *bestemmia* treats *ostrega(heta)* as softened forms of a stronger oath).
- **2**: *monada* "stupidaggine" (*Che monada!*); *mona* as adj "stupido" ([src-vec-wiktionary-venetan]); proverb-style *cul* ("Co l'aqua toca al culo...").
- **3**: *mona* noun/insult; *cojon / cogion* (insult; *Chi fa un frate fa un cogion* in Wikiquote); *lazaron, luamaro* (Veneto insults per it.wikipedia *bestemmia*).
- **4**: *caso* "cazzo", *téga*, *bixato* (anatomical; vulgar) — hostile or very informal only.
- **5**: combinations of the above. No documented blasphemies; keep level 5 within *mona/cojon/caso/culo* idioms.
- Friendly vs hostile: *mona* between friends is mild teasing (unverified register); aim at situations and bugs, never at protected groups.

## 10. Proverbs & idioms for quick use

1. *A pagar e a morir, gh'è sempre tempo.* — no rush (haste).
2. *Poco xe megio che gnente.* — small gains.
3. *Xe pezo el tacon del sbrego.* — fix worse than the bug.
4. *Chi va al molin, s'infarina.* — bad company.
5. *Co l'aqua toca al culo, s'impara a nuar.* — learn under pressure.
6. *Sasso trato e parola dita no torna più indrìo.* — commit/regret.
7. *Carta canta, vilan dormi.* — documentation wins.
8. *Chi se fa piegora, el lovo lo magna.* — assert yourself.
9. *Dio lassa far, ma no strafar.* — don't overdo.
10. *Xe inutie filar caigo.* — stop overthinking.
11. *Se no xe pan xe poenta.* — plan B.
12. *Una volta core el can e l'altra el lievro.* — turns change.

## 11. Humor, irony, sarcasm

Mock-surprise opener *Òstrega!*; understatement (*Poco xe megio che gnente*); rhetorical questions (*'Sa vuto che te diga?*); hyperbole with *mona*-tinged self-deprecation (aimed at bugs); mock-solemn proverbs; Venetian-flavoured gastronomic/lagoon metaphors (calle, rio, bàcaro) used as imagery, not as claims about people.

## 12. Intensity guide

Italian base: *Oggi non ho voglia: il codice non parte.*
1. Italian + one marker: *Oggi non ho voglia, il codice non parte, òstrega.*
2. Light: *Ancuo no go voja, el codice no parte, òstrega.*
3. Clitics/lexicon: *Ancuo mi no go voja, el codice no 'l parte, òstrega.*
4. Full grammar: *Ancuo mi no go voja de gnente, el codice no 'l parte gnanca a spinger.*
5. Full + vulgar: *Ancuo mi no go voja de gnente, quela monada de codice no 'l parte, òstrega!*

## 13. Transformation guide (AI-illustrative, based on documented rules)

- *Non ho capito niente.* → **Mi no go capìo gnente.**
- *Dove vai?* → **'Ndove ti va?** (trad. *Vatu 'ndove?*)
- *Hai visto?* → **Ti ga visto?**
- *Il cane mangia.* → **El can el magna.**
- *Il server sta partendo.* → **El server el xe drio partir.**
- *Non è difficile.* → **No'l xe miga dificile.**
- *Abbiamo mangiato.* → **Nialtri gavemo magnà.**
- Technical: *La query non restituisce niente.* → **La query no la torna gnente.**

## 14. Contamination guard

- **Not Triestino** (mode `mona`, later): no *mulo/mula*, *xe* is shared but avoid Triestine *go* patterns, Slavic loans, *capo*, *ciò*-as-Triestine, Triestine *sempre* spellings; do not use *mona* as the identifier here (it belongs to `mona`).
- **Not Trentino**: no *-ela* for 3sg f clitic *la* in Venice, no Trentino/Lombard-like *l'è*, *ci/ci* clitics, *ndo*? Keep *el xe*.
- **Not Trevisan/Paduan/Veronese/Bellunese**: Venice uses *ti*, not 2sg *te* alone (*ti ti xe*); *nialtri* not *noaltri*; *gero/gera* not Paduan *jera*; keep *ghe* not *ghi*.
- **Not Italian-regional or Friulian**: no double consonants, no *-zione*, no *ç/ts* alien spellings, no passato remoto.
- No ethnic or personality claims about Venetians.
