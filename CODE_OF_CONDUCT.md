# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful to every contributor, whatever their region, language, background or identity. The
project's *content* contains explicit language by design; that never justifies insulting, harassing or
discriminating against people in issues, pull requests or discussions. Regional stereotypes aimed at people are
not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting / security advisories](https://github.com/vapor16/ItalianAccent/security/advisories/new)
or by opening an issue asking a maintainer to contact you. Maintainers will review reports promptly and
confidentially and may remove content or ban participants.
